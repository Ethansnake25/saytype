# 🎙️ saytype - Dictation that just works

## ✨ What is saytype?

saytype is a **magical keyboard shortcut** for your Mac that turns your voice into perfect text, right where you're typing. Press a key, speak naturally in Russian or English, and watch as your words appear with proper punctuation—no internet, no setup, no fuss.

**Imagine this:** You're writing an email, a chat message, or a document. Instead of typing, you just hold a key, say what you want, and it appears instantly. That's saytype.

## ⚙️ How It Works (Simple Version)

- When you speak, saytype listens for just a moment.
- It uses a smart AI model called **Whisper** that understands both Russian and English in the same sentence.
- The AI adds commas, periods, and question marks automatically based on how you speak.
- Everything happens on your Mac's own brain (the Apple Silicon chip), so it's fast and completely offline.

## 🧠 Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Does this work on any Mac?

saytype is designed for **Macs with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, or newer). If you have an older Intel Mac, it might still work, but performance may be slower.

### Q: Will my voice be sent over the internet?

**No, never.** All speech processing happens locally on your computer. Nothing is ever uploaded.

### Q: What if I speak a mix of Russian and English?

Great question! saytype excels at this. It's trained to understand Russian sentences that include English tech terms like "commit," "build," "array," or "button."

### Q: Can I use it in any application?

Yes! Whether you're typing in Safari, Chrome, Microsoft Word, Telegram, Slack, or any other app—if you can type into it, saytype can dictate into it.

### Q: Is it free?

Yes, saytype is completely free and open-source. You can even check the code if you're curious at our GitHub page provided above.

## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

- **Speak clearly** but naturally, just like talking to a friend.
- **Say punctuation** out loud if you want specific control: say *"запятая"* for a comma, *"точка"* for a period.
- **Pause slightly** between sentences to help the AI detect natural breaks.
- **Check backward** if you speak a code term—sometimes the AI might spell it out. For example, saying "C++" might write "Плюс плюс." You can always fix it.

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### saytype doesn't respond to my voice
- Make sure the microphone icon in your menu bar is **green or highlighted** (not gray).
- Click the icon and verify "Start Listening" is on.
- Try speaking a bit louder or closer to your Mac's built-in microphone.

### Text appears in the wrong language
- saytype is tuned to default to Russian with embedded English. If English-only text isn't working, try adding some Russian words to help it understand context, or simply rephrase.
- Make sure you're not switching system keyboard languages mid-sentence.

### The app won't open
- Check if your Mac is blocking it due to "security" settings. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and look for a message about saytype being blocked. Click "Allow Anyway."

### I hold Fn but nothing happens
- Some keyboards have a Fn lock feature. Try pressing Fn once then double-pressing and holding.
- Make sure your keyboard is connected properly (if using an external keyboard).
